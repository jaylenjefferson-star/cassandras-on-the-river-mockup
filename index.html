Meet the chef

Chef Cassandra — “Chef J”

Executive Chef Cassandra opened Cassandra's on the River in Shreveport's Red River District in June 2024, bringing generations of Louisiana cooking to the riverfront. Every plate — from the seafood gumbo to the smothered catfish — is rooted in authentic Cajun and Creole tradition and served with genuine Southern hospitality.

“Come enjoy seafood with us.” It's more than an invitation — it's how the whole team welcomes every guest, every night.
